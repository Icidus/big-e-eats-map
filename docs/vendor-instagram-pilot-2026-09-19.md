Preserve the original source and add supplemental citations if these details are incorporated later.

Visitor comments asking for dairy-free food, a blooming onion, or blueberry donuts were not treated as vendor confirmations. Image alt text helped discover posts but was not used alone to confirm menu availability.

## Feasibility and next step

The pilot verified two vendor accounts and two useful current Big E posts from one vendor. It found no confirmed new Big E menu items. Direct browser inspection succeeded where search-tool Instagram requests failed, so search results alone undercount useful material. Public access and the visible recent-post window limit completeness.

A next batch of ten vendors is reasonable: start with vendors whose catalog menus are sparse. Record account verification, exact post URL, publication date, explicit event/year, food detail, and whether it confirms an existing entry, adds information, or proposes a new item. Require direct vendor or fair evidence of 2026 Big E availability before promoting general menu or other-fair findings into the catalog. This supports small reviewed batches; the pilot does not establish a dependable unattended collection process.

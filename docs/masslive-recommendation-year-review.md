# MassLive recommendation-year correction

Reviewed September 19, 2026 against the two original PDFs in this directory. Text extraction and visual inspection of the relevant pages were used; the source PDFs are unchanged.

## Eater’s Guide

`The Big E Eater’s Guide 2026- What to eat and where to find it.pdf`:

- Page 1: article published September 18, 2026, 6:00 a.m. The introduction identifies it as an update of the annual guide most recently run in 2025.
- Page 23: the recommendation section explicitly reads **“Nick’s picks for the 10 must-try foods at The Big E for 2025.”**
- Page 24: its ten foods match our `masslive-must-try` collection: Fried Butter, Doughco, Push-Up Sushi, Dilly Dilly Dog, Turducken Sandwich, Bacon Waffle Burger, MooNugs, Big Sexy, BBQ Sundae, and Giant Mozzarella Stick. The source says they are in map order, not ranked.

The article’s 2026 publication date does not override that section’s explicit 2025 label. We cannot determine from the saved PDF whether MassLive intended to update the heading. Until there is explicit evidence, the collection must be presented as 2025 picks.

## Opening-day review

`Everything we ate at The Big E- Prices, ratings and our favorites.pdf`:

- Page 1: published September 19, 2026, 5:10 a.m.; describes reporters tasting foods on opening day.
- Page 4: photo caption identifies opening day as September 18, 2026, and starts the top-rated list with Samoa Doughnut.
- Page 5: continues with Maple Jerk Chicken Plate, Del’s Lemonade, Pulled Pork Baked Potato, S’mored Oreo, Alfredo Pasta Bomb, Cheese Dog and Bacalaito, and Apple Nachos.
- Eight numbered entries represent nine individual foods because Cheese Dog and Bacalaito share one entry. All nine match `masslive-opening-day`.

This is verified current-year recommendation evidence. It does not imply that every recommended dish debuted in 2026.

## Correction

- Homepage now leads with **MassLive’s 2026 opening-day favorites**, featuring Samoa Doughnut, Maple Jerk Chicken Plate and S’mored Oreo, and links all nine foods and the original review.
- Older collection explicitly says **MassLive’s 2025 Must-Try Picks** and explains the heading retained in the updated article.
- Existing collection IDs, source citations and catalog listings are preserved so saved links keep working.
- Regression checks require explicit years and prevent the older featured foods from silently returning to the current-year homepage spotlight.
